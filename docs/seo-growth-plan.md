# SEO priorities from the first Search Console impressions

Baseline supplied by the owner: September 2–3, 2026, 23 visible queries,
51 impressions and zero clicks in those query rows. These are not unique visitors
or necessarily property-wide totals. Average positions and page-level results were
not supplied, so this is an intent-based content plan, not a ranking diagnosis.

Internal links now expose /solutions, /industries,
/invoice-reconciliation-software and /accounts-receivable-reconciliation.
Audience breadcrumbs link to their hub pages. Article dates are rendered from
content metadata, with original publication dates retained for revised articles.
The sitemap uses each article's real modification date.

## After publishing

1. In Search Console, confirm the existing sitemap is successful and inspect the two
   new URLs. Request indexing once if appropriate; do not repeatedly submit them.
2. Record publication date. Compare complete post-release periods, initially weekly
   and then a 28-day window, using query AND landing-page filters. Do not compare an
   incomplete day with a full day or interpret one lost click as a trend.
3. Review impressions, clicks, CTR and average position together. Low position calls
   for a relevance/content/discovery review; meaningful impressions at better
   positions with few clicks may justify a title or description test.
4. Compare tool use, signup, first completed reconciliation and paid conversion for
   organic visitors where consent and attribution permit. Keep QA excluded from
   customer reporting. Search Console impressions are not app analytics visits.

## Next editorial work, after page-level evidence

- Distinguish the bookkeeper product/solution/industry pages by real purpose; do not
  add more synonymous pages or redirect existing ones without reviewing their data.
- Add a genuine product walkthrough, operator identity and approved customer case
  studies. Do not invent endorsements, credentials, performance claims or ratings.
- Share useful tools with relevant bookkeeping communities and partners where
  welcome. No bulk link schemes, paid ranking promises or unsolicited mass posting.
